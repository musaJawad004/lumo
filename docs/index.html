Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built to make the daily check-in take seconds, so the habit, not the app, gets your attention.

Morning, Workload, Night

Group habits by part of the day and check them off in one tap. Count habits like "8 glasses" step up as you go.

Streaks that feel fair

Rest days don't break your streak, and an unfinished today never counts against you before it's over.

Stats that motivate

Current and best streak, completion rate, your best weekday, and a per-habit breakdown for the week or month.

Habit history

Every habit has its own page with a progress ring and a 15-week heatmap of how consistent you've been.

Gentle reminders

A daily check-in plus a reminder per habit, on every day or just the weekdays you choose. Always silent.

Synced, even offline

Changes save instantly on your phone and sync to your account. Sign in anywhere and pick up where you left off.

Speaks 10 languages

English, العربية, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Türkçe, हिन्दी, اردو and Bahasa, with right-to-left layouts.

Glass, light & dark

A calm frosted-glass design with Apple's Liquid Glass tab bar on iOS 26, in light and dark mode.

How it works

Start your streak in a minute

1

Sign in

Continue with Google or email. Lumo adds a few starter habits so your first day isn't empty.

2

Shape your day

Add habits to Morning, Workload or Night, pick a daily goal, the days, and an optional reminder.

3

Check in & glow

Tap to log, watch the ring fill, and keep your streak going. Your stats update as you go.

Open source

Built in the open

Lumo is free and open source. Read the code, open an issue, or run your own copy with your own Supabase backend.
