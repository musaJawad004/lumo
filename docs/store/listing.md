## Description
Lumo is a minimal habit tracker that keeps the daily check-in to a few seconds.

**Plan your day**
• Group habits into Morning, Workload and Night
• Tap to check off; count habits like "8 glasses of water" step up as you go
• Run habits every day or only on the days you choose

**Stay motivated**
• Fair streaks: rest days never break them, and an unfinished today never counts against you
• Stats: current and best streak, completion rate, best weekday, weekly and monthly charts
• Every habit gets its own page with a progress ring and a 15-week history heatmap

**Never forget**
• A gentle daily check-in reminder
• Optional reminders per habit, on the days that habit is scheduled

**Yours, everywhere**
• Sign in with Google or email; your habits and settings sync across devices
• Works offline and syncs when you're back online
• Light and dark mode, frosted-glass design
• Available in English, العربية, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Türkçe, हिन्दी, اردو and Bahasa Indonesia

Lumo is free and open source.
